Questions

How much will it cost?

It depends on the parts and the time needed. I give a clear quote after hearing your idea, before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Small builds can take days; bigger ones take longer. You get an estimate with the quote.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Explain what you want in simple words, in Tamil or English, and I will handle the rest.

Can you do my school project?

I'm happy to guide you and explain how a project works so you can learn it, but I don't make work to be handed in as someone else's.
